About

Short version

I work in two places that people usually keep apart. One is a classroom system, the other is a forest edge.

At Teach For Bangladesh I am an Associate on the Alumni Impact team, where I look after a 398 member alumni community, run the training and facilitation, and design the frameworks that tell us whether any of it is working. I came through the Fellowship myself and taught in Kamrangir Char for two years before that.

In 2021 I founded Folklore Expedition Bangladesh, a youth led research and publishing platform that documents oral tradition and folk memory and puts it back in front of people. It has reached more than 1.5 million people, publishes the Bengali periodicals Talpotro and Shoroshboti, and works across ethnic communities on both sides of the border.

My first training was in environmental science, and that is where the thinking behind all of it was built. Stories are how people stay attached to a place. When a river shifts or a forest closes, the stories go first, and they take the knowledge with them. The work lines up with the goals on quality education, gender equality, reduced inequalities, climate action and strong institutions.
